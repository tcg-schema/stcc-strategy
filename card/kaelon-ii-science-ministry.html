Card text

PLAY
Action
Deploy this card. Place 3 Dilithium on this card and exhaust this card.
CLEANUP
If this card is exhausted, gain 1 Research. Otherwise, move 1 Dilithium from this card to a card in the Market. If there is no resources here, log this card.
REACTION
After logging a Person / Directive, you may gain 1 Dilithium from here and you may draw a card from your Discard pile.
