Gain 1 Military. Discard a card from your hand or the the top of your deck. For each different one of Augment / Xindi / Romulan you have in your Discard pile, gain 1 Military.

After your opponent deploys a Ship, either find a Weapon OR send an Away Team to a Location where you have an Away Team.
