Card text

PLAY
Action
Log a card from your hand to gain 3 Dilithium and gain 1 Research / Influence / Military. If the logged card has Military Skill / Military Focus gain 1 (additional) Influence.
RESUPPLY
If you have more Research than Influence, exhaust this card to either: gain 1 Glory OR draw a card.
ACTIVATION
Discard a Research Skill / Research Focus to find any card, except in your Reserve deck.
