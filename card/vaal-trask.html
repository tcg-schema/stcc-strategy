PLAY
Action
You may discard a Starfleet to scan for a Weapon. You may Log a Person from your hand or Discard pile. If you do both, gain 1 Glory, and you may promote this card to Duty Officer.
RESUPPLY
For each Away Team you or your opponent have on a neutral Location, either: discard the top card of your Draw deck OR spend 1 Dilithium to draw a card from your Discard pile.
