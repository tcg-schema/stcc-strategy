PLAY
Free, Attack
Gain 1 Glory and discard the top card of your opponent's Draw deck. IF it is a Person, you both take an Incident.
CLEANUP
For each unspent Action, temporarily increase your hand size by 1 and you may gain 1 Glory.
ACTIVATION
Refresh the I.K.S. Gr'oth. You may discard a card to refresh another Ship with Klingon.
