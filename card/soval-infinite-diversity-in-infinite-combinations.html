Action
You may remove an Away Team from a controlled Location, log a non-Vulcan Person / Cargo / Ally from your Staging Area, and dismiss a deployed Ship. If you do all three, look at the top 2 Encounter. Take on of them and put it on the top of your deck, and return the other to the bottom of its deck.
