Card text

PLAY
Action
Put a card on the top of your deck to gain a card of the same suit from the Junk. Log the gained card and gain 1 Glory. If the gained card has Ancient, draw 2 cards.
ACTIVATION
Gain 1 Latinum. You may discard a Starfleet to gain 1 Latinum.
REACTION
After putting a Cargo / Alien / Ancient into play, spend 1 Latinum to gain 2 Research.
