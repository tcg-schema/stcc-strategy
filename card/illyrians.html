Promote a Person from your hand or the Staging Area to Duty Officer.
PLAY
Action
Discard a Starfleet to scan 2 of Person. Promote the gained card to Duty Officer.

Gain 1 Research for each Duty Officer you have in play. If you have no other Augment in play, log this card.
