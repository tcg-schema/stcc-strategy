Card text

PLAY
Action
Discard a card to deploy this card. If the discarded card is Doctor or Business, gain 2 Latinum and you may free play an Incident.
RESUPPLY
For each Augment you have in play: draw a card, then discard a card. If you have no Person on duty, dismiss this card.
