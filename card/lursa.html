Card text

PLAY
Action
Gain 1 Latinum. You may beam this card to a Ship to send an Away Team to its Location. You may spend 1 Dilithium and take an Incident to gain a ship.
ACTIVATION
Discard a card to draw a card. If the discarded card is a Romulan, gain 1 Military. If it is a Business, gain 1 Latinum. If it has both traits, gain both rewards.
