COST

3 Dilithium.
PLAY
Free, Attack
Deploy this card. Refresh a Ship. Junk a card from the Market. If the opponent has 2 or more Ship deployed, force them to either dismiss on OR take an Incident.
REACTION
After deploying a Ship without Cloak, that ship gains Cloak for the remainder of your turn. Then, you may discard a card to send an Away Team to a Location, ignoring any opponent Ship.
