Action
Refresh up to 3 of your Ship. Draw 2 cards. Junk a card from the Market. Deploy this card.
REACTION
After deploying a Ship without Cloak, that Ship gains Cloak for the remainder of your turn, and you may discard a card to send an Away Team to a Location, ignoring any opponent Ship.
