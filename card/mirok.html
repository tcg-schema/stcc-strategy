PLAY
Free, Attack
You may log a Cloak from your hand or from play to force your opponent to log a Ship from their Discard pile. If they do, gain 1 Glory. If they cannot, they take an Incident. You may promote this card to Duty Officer.
REACTION
After putting a Cargo into play, draw a card.
