Card text

PLAY
Free, Attack
Refresh a Starfleet and force your opponent to discard a card. If your opponent has an Augment in play, repeat this and gain 1 Glory.
RESUPPLY
You may discard a card to draw a card. Repeat this for each Ongoing you have in play.
SPECIAL
While this card is in play (excluding Beamed), your opponent cannot use Reaction operations on your turn.
